The Superpowers You Get

Why Should Every School Student Code? 🌟

Learning to code doesn't mean you have to become a computer programmer. It helps you become a master problem-solver in school and daily life!

🧠

Super Problem Solving

Coding teaches you how to break giant, scary problems into tiny, bite-sized puzzle pieces you can solve easily!

Skill: Logical Thinking
🎨

Unleash Your Creativity

Instead of just playing video games on your phone, you get to CREATE your own video games, animations, stories, and apps!

Skill: Creative Design
🛡️

Never Fear Mistakes!

In coding, mistakes are called "Bugs" 🐞. Every bug you fix makes you smarter and teaches you resilience!

Skill: Debugging Grit
🚀

Build The Future

From flying spacecraft to Mars, inventing robotic arms, to creating medical cures—every future innovation is powered by code!

Skill: Future Ready
